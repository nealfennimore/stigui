1a:T677,Users of conference room or office-based VTC systems and PC-based communications applications that employ a camera must not inadvertently display sensitive or classified information that is not part of the communications session while the camera is active. This can happen if information in the form of charts, pictures, or maps are displayed on a wall within the viewing or capture range of a camera. Any pan, tilt, and zoom (PTZ) capabilities of the camera must be considered. One may consider visual information out of range, but it may be in range considering camera capabilities such as high definition, PTZ, and video enhancement possibilities for captured frames. Inadvertent display of classified information could also happen if the information is lying on a desk or table unprotected.

- Vulnerability awareness and operational training will be provided to users of VTC and video/collaboration communications-related cameras regarding these requirements.
